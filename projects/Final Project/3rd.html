The Importance of Basketball IQ, Basketball IQ is the ability to read the game and create advantages in offense and defense. Reading the game offensively and defensively are some of the important ability of having high Basketball IQ. These are the benefits of having high basketball IQ: Understand Their Role Within a System, Thrive Even When Under Pressure, Maximize Scoring Opportunities, Have Excellent Anticipation on Defense, Know How to Manipulate the Defense, Have Excellent Anticipation on Defense, Amplify Their Team's Success, and Dictate Tempo to Suit Their Team's Advantage
