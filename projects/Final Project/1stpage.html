Beginner Basketball Training

Basketball is a team sport where two teams of five players usually play both offense and defense. The goal is to score more points than the opposing team by using strategies to defend and limit the opponent's scoring while scoring more points to win the game. The game can also be played one-on-one, three-on-three, or in other formats, but not with more than five players on each team. The main objective is to score more points and win the game by using different strategies such as defensive schemes, offensive sets, roster rotation, and time management.

Ball handling

Ball Handling: Learn the "Finger-Tip Feel." Practice low dribbles (at ankle height) and power dribbles (at waist height) to develop control.

Shooting the Ball

Shooting the ball is as vital as dribbling. Having correct form, balance, and follow-through can make your shots more accurate and effective. According to Online-Basketball-Drills, there are basic training steps that players can follow to have proper shooting form and shooting motion. The acronym BEEF stands for Balance, Elbow in, Eyes on the rim, and Follow-through.
